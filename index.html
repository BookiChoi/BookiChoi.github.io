About

My Story

From July 2022 to December 2025, I worked as a Frontend Engineer building production web products at a B2B2C startup.

Over those 3.5 years, I shipped internal tools, client-facing platforms, and embeddable widgets used by businesses and their end users. But the more I built, the more I found myself thinking beyond the code — about the product decisions behind each feature.

I kept coming back to questions like:

  • Why should we build this?
  • What problem does it solve for the user?
  • Will people actually find it valuable?
  • How do we know if it worked?

Working directly with customers along the way — and often proposing solutions myself, whether shaping UX priorities or finding creative workarounds for technical constraints — helped that instinct grow naturally.

Then, around October 2025, the rapid advancement of AI forced me to seriously reconsider my long-term career. Rather than competing on implementation alone, I wanted to contribute earlier in the process — where business problems are defined, requirements are shaped, and decisions are made.

That's why I moved to Vancouver in January 2026 — a place where I could sharpen my English and study Data Analytics at the same time. I'm now preparing for a career as a Business Analyst, combining my engineering background with analytical skills to bridge technology and business decisions.
